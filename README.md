# E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end E-Commerce Sales Analysis project designed to analyze
sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance,
and regional trends using Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify high-performing categories and subcategories
- Identify top-selling products and customers
- Analyze regional and state-level sales performance
- Understand customer segment performance
- Analyze the relationship between discount and profit
- Identify opportunities to improve profitability

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- SQL / MySQL
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- Technology generated approximately 840K in sales and 147K in profit.
- West was the highest-performing region, generating approximately 740K in sales.
- Consumer was the largest customer segment with approximately 1.17M in sales.
- Copiers generated the highest subcategory profit at approximately 56K.
- Tables generated approximately 208K in sales but recorded approximately 18K in negative profit.
- The analysis shows weaker profitability among several highly discounted transactions.
- California generated the highest state-level sales among the states shown, at approximately 460K.

The analysis highlights differences between sales performance and
profitability across products, categories, regions, and customer segments.
The dashboard can help identify high-performing areas as well as
